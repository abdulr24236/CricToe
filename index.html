    How to play CricToe

    1. Every day gives you a fresh 3×3 grid: three row categories across the top, three column categories down the side.
    2. Tap a square and name a cricketer who matches both its row and its column. Every tile shows a picture plus what it means.
    3. You start with 3:00 on the clock. The timer starts the moment you tap your first square.
    4. A correct answer locks the square and adds +10s. A wrong answer costs −15s and tells you which of the two categories you missed.
    5. Fill all nine squares to win and extend your streak. If the clock runs out, every remaining square is revealed.

    What each category means

    • Country, format and role — e.g. India, Test cricket, All-rounder.
    • Leagues — the cricketer has played at least one match in that league.
    • Trophies — they were part of a winning squad, not only the final XI.
    • Stats — e.g. 400+ caps means 400 or more international appearances.
    • “Played with…” — a tile showing a cricketer's name and photo means the answer must have appeared in the same XI as that player — for their country, or as a franchise teammate in the IPL, PSL, BBL, CPL, The Hundred, ILT20 or SA20. Playing against them does not count, and neither does being captained by them.

    Worked example: the square where “Played ODI cricket” meets “India” is anyone who has played an ODI for India — Virat Kohli or Rohit Sharma, to name two.

    Stuck? Hit Give up & reveal for one valid answer per square, or switch on Practice for an untimed board that leaves your streak alone.
